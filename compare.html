Who builds on whom

Solid lines: one is built on the other. Dashed: it copies the other’s API or syntax, or was made by the same person. The boxes on the right hold the four that stand alone; Astro is the one that hosts the others’ components, as the list below says.

  • Next.js is built on React; Nuxt on Vue.
  • Preact has React’s API; Solid has its JSX.
  • Qwik and Angular share an author, Miško Hevery.
  • Astro hosts components written in Preact, Svelte, Lit and others.

Where they overlap, where they differ

They overlap on

  • Components. Every one here builds screens from pieces, except htmx, which adds behaviour to HTML.
  • Reactivity. Vue, Solid, Svelte, Angular and Preact all offer reactive values, under the name of refs, signals or runes.
  • Templates. Vue, Svelte and Angular write HTML-like templates; React, Solid, Preact and Qwik write JSX.
  • A meta-framework on top. Next.js, Nuxt, and the SvelteKit, SolidStart and Qwik City layers.

They differ on

  • Who does the work. Svelte at build time, React at run time, htmx on the server, Qwik as late as possible.
  • How much ships. From one script for htmx to a framework for Angular: see the sizes below.
  • How much you choose. React leaves the router to you; Angular supplies one.
  • What a change re-runs. A whole component in React, a single expression in Solid, nothing in Astro pages without islands.
